# Trailpost status, 2026-10-08

Where things stand before a few days off. Trailpost is a fan-made trading hub
for WalkScape: people post listings and make offers here, then do the swap in
the game's trade window. Built 2026-10-02 to 10-08: 38 PRs, about 13k lines of
Elm, production on Evergreen V31. Trading went live in WalkScape on 10-07.

## Architecture

- **Lamdera**, Elm on both sides. `Backend.elm` timestamps every request
  (`FromFrontendAt`). A 15s tick puts listings live once their 5-minute wait
  is up. Listing, offer and trade rules live in `Market.elm` and price
  estimates in `Pricing.elm`; both sides use them, so the frontend and backend
  can't disagree. One module per screen in `Page/*`.
- **Identity:** Discord OAuth is the only sign-in. Your public identity is the
  WalkScape character name you claim: first-come, case-insensitive, shown as
  UNVERIFIED, with look-alike detection.
- **Items:** 708 tradeable items and their icons, generated from the WalkScape
  Tools API by `scripts/import-items.py` and `scripts/pull-icons.py`. Each
  variant (fine, rare egg, crafted quality) has its own price series.
- **Ops:**
  - Merging to `main` deploys to production.
  - Each PR gets a preview app in one of 5 fixed slots (`trailpost-pr-a..e`),
    signed in through the staging Discord app.
  - The Evergreen staleness check runs in the pre-push hook, CI and
    `deploy.sh`.
  - CI also runs elm-review and the program-test E2E suite.
  - Analytics go to Simple Analytics through a port.
- **Can't do:** `lamdera backend` only reads a local model, not production.
  Production state is only visible through `/admin`.

## What's shipped

- **Onboarding:** Discord → claim your character name (spaces allowed,
  look-alikes flagged).
- **Listings:**
  - Buy or sell, with fine/rare/quality variants.
  - Payment in coins, items only, or coins or items. A listing that takes
    items can name up to 5 it wants.
  - A 5-minute wait with a countdown before a listing goes live.
  - Up to 20 active listings each.
  - Price pages can pre-fill a new listing.
- **Offers:** always for the full quantity, with up to 5 item lines.
- **Trade lifecycle:** accepting an offer marks the listing "trade pending" and
  shows both sides a checklist: who invites whom, what each side puts in, and
  the things to check before Accept (fine vs. normal color, coin digits,
  look-alike names). Then both say "It went through", or either says "It fell
  through" with a reason and the listing goes back up.
- **Prices:** with 3+ confirmed trades in 30 days, the estimate is their
  median. Otherwise it comes from asks, bids and offers (one vote per trader
  per day, outliers cut). The chart was redesigned for items with few points.
- **Trust:** each profile shows a trade record ("12 trades · 0 fell through")
  and the Discord account's age. Reports can include a copy of the trade and up
  to 3 screenshots.
- **Admin:** reports, deleting listings and offers, bans, releasing names, and
  an audit log.

## Known debt

- Screenshots sit in the BackendModel (up to ~1.2MB per report). Fine at 0
  reports. Delete them when a report is resolved if that changes.
- Admins are matched by Discord username, not id.
- Bans don't stick for preview accounts (moot in production, where preview
  sign-in is off).
- Listings never expire and can't be edited.

## Next

In rough order:

1. **Edit listings.** The most obvious missing piece. Editing should restart
   the 5-minute wait.
2. **Chat on accepted trades.** The most useful trust and coordination
   feature: agreeing on a time to be online, fixing a mismatch before Accept.
   There's an empty `chat-offers-public-add` workspace (phoenix) to start from.
3. **Import your bank.** WalkScape can export your character as JSON. Another
   trading site uses it so you can see your duplicates and what other people
   have for chest-item trades. Worth copying: it would also make posting a
   listing one click.
4. **Notifications** when someone offers on your listing or accepts your
   offer (Discord DM or browser push). People are out walking.
5. **Listing expiry** with a "Still available?" bump, before stale listings
   pile up.
6. Wanted-items follow-ups: an offer that starts from the wanted items, and
   market search that matches them.

Dropped for now:

- **Name verification.** Nobody seems worried about name grabbing, and trades
  swap both sides at once.
- **Escrow.** Trust is high and the game already makes the swap atomic.

## Side projects

- **Staging export/import** (bandung, `elliotfiske/staging-export/import-plan`,
  plan in `.context/plans/`). Makes `trailpost-staging` a real thing (config,
  scripts, deploys from a scratch clone, `AppEnv.isStaging`). Adds
  authenticated RPCs to export production data and load it into staging, so
  Evergreen migrations can be rehearsed on real data. Should carry over to
  future Lamdera projects, which makes it worth doing well.

## The way out

If keeping a trading site going turns out to be too much work, Trailpost
could become a price-estimate site only: track prices from the Discord's Grand
Emporium and other trading sites instead of hosting listings. The price pages
and `Pricing.elm` already do most of it. No trade lifecycle, reports or bans
to look after.
